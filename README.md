# AI Research Intelligence & Research Paper Evaluation Platform

## Project Philosophy

This platform provides evidence-grounded research decision support.

It is not an autonomous scientific authority and does not claim to definitively determine scientific truth, publication acceptance, plagiarism, or absolute research novelty.

AI-generated assessments should be supported by evidence, sources, reasoning, and uncertainty wherever possible.
